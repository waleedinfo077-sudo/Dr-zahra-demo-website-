Her story

Clinical dermatology, explained like a person.

Dr. Sameen Zahra trained in dermatology in the United States and holds a Master of Public Health. That combination shapes how she practises: treat what is in front of her, and prevent what will come next.

She founded Solace Medical Centre for medical dermatology and Solace Aesthetics for cosmetic care, so patients get the right room for the right concern — and never a procedure they do not need.

Outside the clinic she teaches a community of more than 91,000 people on Instagram and YouTube, mostly by debunking things they were told to buy.

01 — Philosophy

How the clinic works

Four principles that decide what gets prescribed — and what does not.

  • Listen first

    The first ten minutes are yours. History, triggers and what you have already tried matter more than a product list.

  • Evidence over trends

    If the published evidence is thin, I will say so — even when a treatment is popular.

  • Skin tone matters

    Peels, lasers and pigmentation plans are chosen for your skin tone, because the same settings do not suit everyone.

  • Prevent before treat

    Sunscreen, sleep and a stable barrier do more over a decade than any single procedure.
