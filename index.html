03 — How it works

Three steps, no surprises

The same consultation-first process at both clinics.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We go through your history, triggers, current products and goals — then look closely at your skin, in good light.

  2. 02

    Your plan

    You leave with a written, staged plan: what to use, what to stop, what it costs, and how long it should realistically take.

  3. 03

    Review

    Follow-ups happen at set intervals. We adjust the plan based on how your skin responds, not on a fixed script.

First consultations usually last 30–45 minutes. Bring your current products and any past prescriptions.

Message the clinic
Do I need a consultation before any treatment?

Yes. Every plan starts with an assessment of your skin, history and goals. It protects you from treatments that won't help and lets us set honest expectations.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment, your skin and how it responds. You'll leave the consultation with an estimated number of sessions and the total cost before you commit.

Is there downtime?

Some treatments cause mild redness, tightness or flaking for a few days. Your clinician will tell you exactly what to expect and how to care for your skin afterwards.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your current products, a list of any medication or supplements, and any previous prescriptions or test results. Photos of past flares are genuinely useful.
