03 — Getting started

Three rules that save skin

  • One new thing at a time

    Introduce a single active, use it for two weeks, then decide. Stacking three at once makes reactions impossible to trace.

  • Sunscreen is the treatment

    For pigmentation and post-acne marks, SPF matters more than the serum. Reapply, and use enough.

  • Stop if it stings

    A brief tingle can be normal. Burning, swelling or blistering is not — stop and message the clinic.
