DR. SAMEEN ZAHRA WEBSITE
Open index.html (or run: python3 -m http.server).

IMAGES: put your photos in assets/images/ using these names. Until then, labelled placeholders show.
  <page>-1.jpg is the hero (doctor portrait, face on the right side)
  <page>-2.jpg is the split-section photo (home, about only)
  <page>-3.jpg to <page>-8.jpg are the gallery images
  <page> = index, about, treatments, products, education, contact
Portrait photos work best (about 4:5). Hero: at least 1600px tall.

CONTENT: edit js/data.js (treatments, products, videos, reviews, FAQ, WhatsApp number).
Replace XXXX placeholders (phone, address) in the HTML files, or edit build.py and run: python3 build.py
